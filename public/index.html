How the score works

Domain age

Very new registrations can raise risk. Missing registration data is treated as an unknown signal.

SSL certificate

We check whether a valid certificate protects the connection. HTTPS alone does not prove a store is legitimate.

Storefront signals

We look for contact details, policy links, and pressure-selling language in the page we can inspect.

75–100 Likely safe 45–74 Use caution 0–44 High risk

Signals can be incomplete for JavaScript-heavy stores, privacy-redacted domains, and new legitimate businesses. Review the evidence before making a purchase decision.
